  5. On submit, OTP is consumed. Regenerate if lost.

DISC Reference Guide

DISC describes observable work style, not capability.

D

Dominance

How people respond to problems & challenges. High-D: direct, decisive, results-oriented, competitive. Low-D: cooperative, low-key, reflective.

I

Influence

How people influence others. High-I: outgoing, persuasive, enthusiastic. Low-I: reserved, analytical.

S

Steadiness

How people pace themselves & respond to change. High-S: patient, dependable, consistent.

C

Conscientiousness

How people respond to rules & procedures. High-C: precise, analytical, systematic.

Understanding the three graphs

Graph I — Work Mask: How the person believes they need to behave at work. Derived from "Most" selections.
Graph II — Behaviour Under Pressure: The instinctive self that emerges under stress. Derived from "Least" selections.
Graph III — Self-Image: The integrated, perceived self. The net pattern of Most minus Least.
